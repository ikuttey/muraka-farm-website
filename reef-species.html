A living field guide

Learn what lives on our house reef.

Dharavandhoo is surrounded by coral reef habitat that supports reef fish, turtles, sharks, rays, invertebrates and diverse coral communities. This guide is designed for students, snorkellers, divers, researchers and anyone who wants to understand the reef beyond simply seeing it.

Muraka Farm will continue expanding this page as field observations and photographs are verified. Where species-level identification is uncertain, we use the most reliable taxonomic level rather than over-identifying from a photograph.

Observe responsibly

Look closely. Leave the reef unchanged.

Do not touch or stand on coral. Even brief contact can damage living tissue.

Give animals space. Do not chase, surround or block their movement.

Photograph, don't collect. Images and field notes are more valuable when the animal remains where it belongs.
